# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Vendor Name**: Spotmies LLP
**Client**: MyBodyQode (MBQ)

## 1. Uptime Guarantee
The Vendor guarantees a **99.5%** uptime for the reporting portal and associated API services, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

## 2. Turnaround Time
The turnaround time from Lab data receipt to report generation is **24 hours** (or less) under normal operating conditions.

## 3. Support & Incident Response
- **Critical Bug-fix Response Time**: 4 hours
- **Non-critical Support Response Time**: 24 hours (or up to 2 days as negotiated)

## 4. Maintenance Windows
Scheduled maintenance will be communicated at least 48 hours in advance and will occur during off-peak hours to minimize disruption.
