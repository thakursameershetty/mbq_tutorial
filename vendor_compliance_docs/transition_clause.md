# Transition & Handover Clause

In the event of contract termination or project handover, Spotmies LLP ("Vendor") agrees to the following transition obligations:

1. **Source Code & IP Handover**: Vendor will transfer all source code, assets, and proprietary algorithms (Qode-scoring logic) developed for MBQ.
2. **Database & Infrastructure**: Vendor will provide complete database dumps and transfer ownership of all cloud infrastructure credentials (e.g., Azure accounts) to the MBQ team.
3. **Knowledge Transfer**: Vendor will provide up to 2 weeks of knowledge-transfer and handover sessions to the incoming MBQ team to ensure smooth continuity of operations.
4. **Data Purge**: Upon confirmation of successful handover, Vendor will securely purge any remaining MBQ data from its internal systems in compliance with the DPDPA.
